## Near misses

- 0x02157290 reset_network_vars: every instruction but 4 matches; the difference is scheduling at
  +0x10..+0x34. The image puts `str r0,[r2,#0x4c]` (the reason store) in the load delay slot
  after `ldr r1,[r1,#0x50]` and emits `mov r5,#0` late, next to `mov r3,#1`. Our build hoists
  `mov r5,#0` into that slot. About 25 variants tried (listed in the file's blocker line).
- 0x0215C910 CPS_Resolve: every instruction but 6 matches; there are two scheduling swaps in the
  id setup. The image loads the pool base before `mov r1,#0`, and stores the new LCG x after the
  first id's `orr`. The rest is exact, including the unfolded 64-bit shift, which comes from
  `(u16)rand32(ctx, 0x10000)` (the 64-bit LCG inline, max 0x10000).

# Wave 11 lane: pkt_02081E7C, pkt_02083028, pkt_02083298

Result: 1 EXACT (796 bytes). 8 addresses turned out not to be functions: they are the inside of SDK functions.

## Identification (callers and libmatch's symbols_sdk.json)
- **0x02081E7C** is the SHA-1 compression function of TwlSDK libmath, from the dgt_sha1 family. Its callers are MATHi_SHA1ProcessBlockForOverlay (a `bl` at 0x020812FC) and MATHi_SetOverlayTableMode (a pool word at 0x020813D4). It is not MD5 and not game code. The K constants sit in the 16 bytes before the entry, at 0x02081E6C.
- **0x02082F48, 0x02082F90, 0x02083028, 0x020830C0** are interior kernels of **MI_CpuCopy**. MI_CpuCopy starts at 0x02082EA4, is 752 bytes, and libmatch has all 752 as concrete bytes.
- **0x02083250, 0x02083298, 0x02083334, 0x020833D0** are interior kernels of **MI_CpuMove**. MI_CpuMove starts at 0x02083194, is 788 bytes, and libmatch has 785 of 788 as concrete bytes.
- Both functions are TwlSDK libmi mi_memory.o hand-written `asm` functions. Each kernel is reached from inside its parent, pops without pc, and falls into a shared tail.
- The wave-7 note called 0x02083298 onward "MSL memmove". That is corrected here: the library is libmi.

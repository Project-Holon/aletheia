## The finding that closed them
The PART-2 composer in cluster_02066740 and all of cluster_020640DC is
**NitroSystem g2d**, and someone has already decompiled NitroSystem:
**github.com/ntrtwl/NitroSystem**, rev 21c8fd0 ("NitroSystem-071126", the
version pret/pokeplatinum builds against). Earlier lanes had spent three
waves on register rotations. I copied the library's own shape: every helper
as the NNS inline it is, locals in the library's scopes, the NitroSDK
GXOamAttr union, and the real TwlSDK inlines. Two functions matched on the
first compile. The third needed one struct-size fix. The permuter could never
have found these fixes because it only changes the `--func` body and never
touches inline boundaries.

## Lessons
- Three of the four "permuter-flat" residues here were in the declarations: a callee's arity (func_020788D8), callee parameters (func_020729A4 / func_02072974), and the callback's argument roles. A field loaded into r0 just before a guarded `bl` is almost always the callee's argument.
- A pool word naming an unlabelled leaf inside the cluster is closed by filing the leaf itself EXACT first. That gives the `matched` class, but only when the caller's source sits inside the repo tree (scratch files get src=None).

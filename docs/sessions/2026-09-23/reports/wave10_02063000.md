## Findings recorded
- **The "mla destination is not source-addressable" rule is refuted as a general rule.** It fell on 0x02063294 through the split-assign lever. It still holds where the image does not coalesce base and destination (378 and the first block of 640). There the image colours base, then destination, then index, and every compile does the reverse.
- **Recurring lever: inline helpers.** Writing guard, lookup and range-check idioms as `static inline` helpers is what fixed the 2.0/sp2 scheduling and allocation in 360/830/948/AFC/434/138.
- data_020A8220.. is a driver/binder handler table: tag words plus function pointers.
- The notes appended are: pkt_02063078.c.txt (378 and 294), func_02063640.c.txt, func_0206343C_and_02063640.c.txt (343C, first source), plus closure lines in sub_02063138, func_02070360 and func_02070AFC. Each carries a best-source marker and a permuter result line.

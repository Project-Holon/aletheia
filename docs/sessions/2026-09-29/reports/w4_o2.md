## Near miss

- ov009 0x0219BED8 qr_build_split_query_reply: one instruction short (632 B against 636 B).
  At the top of the key-type loop body, the object reloads `progress->mCurKeyType`
  (`ldr r0, [r8]`) for the key_list_callback argument after the numkeys test. This build
  reuses the loop-test value, so numkeys goes to r1 instead of r0, and every later pool
  offset and branch moves by 4. Everything else is byte-equal, including
  `packetNumPos = &buf->buffer[buf->len++]; *packetNumPos = ...` and the final `|= 0x80`.
  Tried: int, enum and cast field and parameter types; while, for, do/while and if+goto
  loop shapes; the key type increment in the for header; a named block local; the call
  moved into the loop head; `volatile` on the field (it reloads everywhere and comes out
  12 B too long). Next idea: something in the original that ends CSE at the body head,
  such as an assert/debug macro that expands to a call-like barrier.

## Evidence and shape notes

- Callees in ov009 carry `ov009`. The gsXml*/gsiExecuteSoap* callees of the ov175 rows
  are matched ov009 files and carry `ov009` on the ov175 headers. ov175 callees
  (sciInterfaceSet*, wsLoginCertWriteXML, the sciWs callbacks) carry `ov175`. ARM9
  callees (OS_SPrintf, memset, memcpy, rand, srand, strlen, atoi, strncmp) carry no token.
- The open-SDK callees (enum_local_ips, get_sockaddrin, sendto, QEStartQuery,
  wsLoginCertWriteXML, and the two SBServerParseQR2FullKeys rows) attest as `sdk`
  through their catalog rows.
- SBServerParseQR2FullKeysSplit calls strncasecmp through the ARM9 veneer at 0x0209ABEC.
  The veneer resolves under the existing `veneer` class, so no new evidence was needed.
- Prototype disagreements (DWARF against the object):
  - sciWsCreateSessionCallback: DWARF lists 3 parameters, but the object reads the
    response from r2 and the user data from r3. An unused word sits in r1, the same as in
    the matched sciWsSetReportIntentionCallback.
  - sciWsSetReportIntention and sciWsSubmitReport: DWARF leaves a one-word gap between
    theCallback and theUserData (fb+52/60 and fb+64/72). I spelled it as an unused
    `theTimeoutMs` parameter.
  - qr2_init_socketA: the qr2_ipverify_info_s element needs word alignment, or ipverify
    lands at 0x119 instead of 0x11C.
- Levers used:
  - send_heartbeat: `lastheartbeat = current_time(); lastka = lastheartbeat;`. The chained
    assignment stores the fields in the wrong order.
  - sciWsCreateSessionCallback: `if (!MoveToStart) r = 9; else if (!Next(a) && !Next(b)) r = 9;`.
    Separate statements keep the per-path `moveq r8, #9`, and a `||` chain merges them.
  - ParseSingleQR2Reply: the split and single branches each get their own
    `state |= 0x43`. The compiler cross-jumps them into the object's `popne` / `b` shape.
  - SBServerParseQR2FullKeysSingle: `memcpy(&nunits, data, 2)` from
    `../arm9_sdk/libc/string.h` inlines as the ldrb/strb pair. `k = data` / `v = data`
    go after the dlen check, and `nkeys++` goes between `data +=` and `len -=`.
  - sakeiFillSoapRequestFieldValues: an if/else chain in the order 0,1,2,9,3,4,5,6,7,8.
    Each close tag repeats its open-tag extern, and the pool dedupes it.

- moveFocus (0x021CD018, 240 B): the four DWCi_Flag results must be evaluated inline as call arguments (`R[func(1)][cur].left, ...`), not stored in locals; locals and every declaration or statement order left 36 B differing (register f4 landed in r2 instead of r3, `cur` in r3 instead of ip, pool words swapped).

SHIFTED ANGLE VALUES ARE NAMED LOCALS LIVE ACROSS THE WHOLE BODY.  The image
spends `mov r0, fp` / `mov r1, r8` at 0x02072390/0x02072394 and again
`mov r0, r8` / `mov r1, fp` at 0x020723F4/0x020723F8, i.e. it computes
`f2c << 12` into r8 and `f2e << 12` into fp ONCE, before the first
func_0207C930, and re-feeds the SAME two registers to the second call with the
arguments swapped.  That is what forces the ten-register
`push {r3, r4, r5, r6, r7, r8, sb, sl, fp, lr}` (`f8 4f 2d e9`).

Spelling the two calls inline --

    func_0207C930((s32)s->f2e << 12, (s32)s->f2c << 12);
    ...
    func_0207C930((s32)s->f2c << 12, (s32)s->f2e << 12);

-- makes the compiler RE-READ f2c/f2e and re-shift at the second call (`ldrh
r0,[r8,#0x2c]` / `ldrh r1,[r8,#0x2e]` / two `lsl`s at +0x78..+0x84), which
frees sl and fp and gives an EIGHT-register push: still 204 bytes, but 43
concrete bytes out.  Binding them:

    f12 = (s32)s->f2c << 12;
    e12 = (s32)s->f2e << 12;
    func_0207C930(e12, f12);
    ...
    func_0207C930(f12, e12);

2. THE REGISTER SWAP IS DECLARATION ORDER.  With the pin in force the whole
residue is 13 instructions of r4 <-> r5.  The fix is
docs/idioms/declaration-order-decides-which-register-a-local-gets.md:
`u32 idx;` must be declared at the TOP of the function, BEFORE
`struct Inner *o;`, rather than inside the `a1 != -1` arm where the note's
shape puts it.  That is what assigns the inner object to r5 and the bit
index to r4, matching the image's `ldr r5, [r3]` at 0x02067DDC and its
`ldr r4, [r5, #0x14]` at 0x02067E30.  Binding a1 to its own local declared
first does NOT do it (still 14 out); only hoisting `idx` does.  With the
pin and the hoist together: 232 bytes, 0 mismatches.

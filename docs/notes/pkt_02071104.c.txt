  * THE REDUCTION IS `>> 8`, NOT `>> 12`.  The image's `lsr r4, ip, #8` /
    `orr r4, r4, r3, lsl #24` at 0x02071130/0x02071134 is the 8-bit form of
    the family's narrowing fold; the usual `>> 12` gives `lsr #0xc` +
    `lsl #20`.  Reading the shift off the `lsl` amount (32 - shift) is the
    quickest check.
  * THE f34 NEGATION IS THREE STATEMENTS (L3/L-H).  `m = f1c << 1; m = 0 - m;
    m = m + 0x2000;`.  Folded, the compiler emits one `rsb r1, r3, #0x2000`

    four-byte size gap and is the only SIZE lever here.

    `(s64)f1c * f28`.  permuter `commute(*)`.

    THE f1c SHIFT'S (`u = f1c << 1; m = u;`).  permuter `temp` x2.

WHAT THIS PASS CHANGES ABOUT THE FAMILY'S STANDING CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This note, docs/notes/pkt_02071DD4.c.txt and docs/notes/pkt_02072374.c.txt all
concluded that the multiply-class register swap "is chosen after linearisation
and is not source-addressable".  THAT IS TOO STRONG, and two rows proved it
this pass: 0x02072374 sat in the class at 7-8 across three lanes and fell to a
single statement reorder (moving the `d->f00` store DOWN past the two
narrowings), and 0x02072068 sat in it at 16 and fell to a single live-range
split.  Both were found by tools/permuter.py in one hill pass each after hand
sweeps had exhausted.

The corrected rule, now written into docs/families/cluster_02071EAC.h as L-J:
treat a member in this class as OPEN, run the permuter (hill first, it is
deterministic and exhausts the singles; anneal after), and only THEN record a
floor.  0x02071104 at 6 and 0x020712A8 at 4 have both had that treatment and
are recorded as floors on that basis, not on a hand sweep's.

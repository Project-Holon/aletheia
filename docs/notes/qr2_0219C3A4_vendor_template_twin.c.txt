WHAT WAS NOT TRIED, AND WHY
---------------------------
Re-spelling the local as a copy from a declared extern
(`memcpy(natNegBytes, g_..., 6)` with `g_... 0x021A1B64 data`) does resolve
the site - `attest()` returns `('sole-ref', 1, 0x021A1B64, 'self')` - because
the ROM word at +0198 already names the address.  Two reasons it is not the
answer:

  * `sole-ref/self` is the weakest class there is: its whole content is that
    the word under comparison agrees with the word being written.  Trading the
    `template` rule (vendor object + image, refused only on uniqueness) for
    self-agreement is a loss, and it would need recording in the shard as
    `evidence: {"sole-ref": ["self:0x021A1B64"]}`.
  * it is not byte-identical.  The memcpy form diffs in 75 concrete bytes:
    qrec moves to r8, the six `ldrb` land in a different register set, and the
    six `strb` come out in source order rather than interleaved with the
    gsDebugFormat setup.  Recovering the original register allocation from a
    re-spelled source is body rewriting, which is exactly what a witness hunt
    is not for.

no tools/mwccarm build writes that stamp, yet sdkobj locate() reports 0 concrete
mismatches of 399 and all 399 reproduce under dsi/1.1p1 -O4,p.  The stamp is

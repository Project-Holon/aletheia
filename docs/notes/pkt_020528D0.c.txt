WHAT IT IS: the texture-upload pass of the material list.  GX_BeginLoadTex,
then a walk of o->f32 rows of 0x14 bytes at o->f2c; each row's first word is a
resource pointer.  A bit of the resource's second word decides between (a)
copying the VRAM handle from ANOTHER row, indexed by an 8-bit field of the
same word, and (b) allocating through the caller's callback and uploading with
GX_LoadTex.  Then GX_EndLoadTex and `return 1`.

COMPILER PIN, measured: ../1.2/sp2.  The interworking-conflict tell,
`blx fp` at 0x02052A08 (`3b ff 2f e1`) beside the ARMv4T-style return
`pop {r4, r5, r6, r7, r8, sb, sl, fp, lr}` / `bx lr` at 0x02052A48/0x02052A4C
(`f0 4f bd e8` / `1e ff 2f e1`), which no dsi front end emits under
-proc arm946e.  Numbers on the identical C: 1.1p1 -> 44 concrete bytes out,
../2.0/sp2 -> 44, ../1.2/sp2 -> 12.  Use --cc ../1.2/sp2 here.

FACTS THAT COST TIME, do not re-derive:

 * o->f32 is a u16 at struct offset 0x32, so the padding after the f2c
   pointer is a single u16, not a u32.  Getting it wrong gives ldrh #0x34
   or #0x36 and reads as a whole-function diff.
 * GX_LoadTex's first argument is `(char *)*(void **)r + 0x20`, i.e. the ROW's
   first word plus 0x20 -- NOT `res->f00 + 0x20`.  The extra dereference costs
   one `ldr` and shifts the tail.  Its second argument is the callback's
   return value and its third is res->f08: (void *, u32, void *).
 * the `lsl #0x1c` / `lsr #0x1c` pair on the field compared against 5 is a
   4-bit UNSIGNED bitfield and must be spelled `(u32)(f << 28) >> 28`; an
   `& 0xf` folds to a single `and r0, r1, #0xf` and loses four bytes
   (docs/idioms/shift-pair-with-no-matching-type-is-a-bitfield.md).
 * the two stack slots at [sp] and [sp,#4] hold the constants 1 and 0, and the
   image assigns them AFTER the zero-trip guard, in the order
   `mov r0,#1` / `mov r6,sb` / `str sb,[sp,#4]` / `str r0,[sp]`.  A plain
   `while` hoists the `off = i` copy ABOVE the `cmp`; only the guarded
   do-while below sinks it to the right place.  That fix alone moved the
   residue from 15 mismatches to 12.

Two independent swaps, same length in every case:
  (1) the byte offset `off` and the row address `r` trade r6 and r8.  The
      image keeps the OFFSET in r6 (the register it also increments by 0x14)
      and the ROW ADDRESS in r8; 1.2 does the reverse.
  (2) the resource's flags word and its first shift trade r0 and r1.  The
      image loads the word into r0 and shifts INTO r1; 1.2 loads into r1 and
      shifts into r0.

None of the ten moves the pair of swaps.  Next thing to try is a spelling that
makes the ROW ADDRESS, not the offset, the loop-carried induction variable

variant 8 and is worse, so it needs the pointer to be the thing INCREMENTED,

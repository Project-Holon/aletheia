Lane O39: ov016 contested data, the remaining ARM band
======================================================

Rule 8 holds unchanged on this lane, and the *first* finding is that most of the
band needs no data symbol at all: 15 of the 17 functions filed here declare only
their own name.  A literal address is not a relocation site (`match.py one`
prints "0 relocation site(s)"), so the pool word is compared byte for byte.
`extern int func_XXXXXXXX[];` plus a token-less `data` symbols line was not
needed once on this lane -- the one case that demanded it on O35 (func_021BD3CC)
stays the worked example, and the fold it fixes (a large literal split across
`add` immediates) is reachable only when the source spells `ADDR + off` as one
literal cast.

Two mechanics this band does need, both measured.

1. KEEP THE BASE LITERAL AND THE OFFSET APART.
   `*(unsigned short *)(0x021DE5EC + 4)` is folded by the compiler into the single
   pool word 0x021DE5F0 and every store then comes out at a negative offset:
   `strh r4,[r0]` / `strb r4,[r0,#-4]` / `strb r4,[r0,#-2]` against the image's
   `strh r4,[r0,#4]` / `strb r4,[r0]` / `strb r4,[r0,#2]`.  Going through a
   pointer local, `unsigned char *g = (unsigned char *)0x021DE5EC;` then
   `*(unsigned short *)(g + 4)` and `*(unsigned char *)(g + 8)`, keeps 0x021DE5EC
   as the pool word and the offset in the instruction.  This cost four functions
   their first compile: func_021C86B8, func_021C8C50, func_021C9D10, func_021D2F50,
   func_021D2F8C and func_021D308C all had `[rX,#4]`/`[rX,#8]` folded away until
   they were rewritten this way.  Brief rule 4 covers stores; it applies equally to
   loads and to halfword access.

2. THE INT LITERAL, NOT THE ADDRESS, TAKES r4 IN THIS COMPILER.
   A whole class of rows in this band is block-for-block right, every relocation
   resolved, and differs only in which callee-saved register holds what.  The
   image puts the base address / global pointer in r4 and the integer constant in
   r5 or r6; the compiler puts the integer constant in r4 and the address in the
   higher register.  Ten rows:
     func_021D298C 10B   func_021D45E0 10B   func_021D6EA4 10B
     func_021BD138  6B   func_021BD704  6B   func_021D45A4  6B
     func_021D28B4  6B   func_021D5904 25B   func_021D5580 16B
     func_021D40F4 14B
   Five spellings were compiled against func_021BD138 (the smallest of them) to
   find a lever and none moved the allocation: a `volatile` pointer, a `char *`
   instead of `unsigned char *`, the index through an `int` local, a fresh literal
   cast per use with no pointer local at all, and the constant replaced by bare
   literal arguments.  All five stay exactly 6 bytes out with the same swapped
   pair.  Register naming of this shape is not addressable from the source at
   this optimisation level; park these as MATCH rather than grinding them.

3. ISA OF AN OUT-OF-REGION CALLEE IS A SEPARATE CAP FROM THE CONTESTED-DATA ONE.
   `func_0207C958` (FX_DivS32, the only unresolved site on this lane) is recorded
   `thumb` by match.py's check even though `layout/symbols_sdk.json` carries
   `thumb: false` for 0x0207C958 and the call site is a plain ARM BL
   (0xEBFACB0B, not BLX).  Declared `arm` the compile stops with "ISA conflict";
   declared `thumb` the bytes come out right and the site is left unresolved for
   want of a witness for the odd address.  The residue is a tool-side ISA/witness
   disagreement, not a source defect -- func_021C9D10 is parked in src/pending
   with every concrete byte correct.

Corrected scale, re-measured after O35's 21 filings
---------------------------------------------------
`scratch/ov_ov016/o39/census.py` rebuilds the band from asm/overlays/ov016.s and
drops everything already in src/matched/ or src/pending/:
  301 ov016 functions still reference a contested address, 251 ARM and 50 thumb.
The 50 includes 35 rows O35's rows2.json called ARM that the listing's own
`.arm`/`.thumb` directive state makes Thumb (e.g. func_021BC804, func_021B7098,
func_021BC7B8): they are Thumb by instruction encoding (2-byte `push {lr}`,
`bl` with `FF F7 ..`), so rows2.json's ISA column was wrong on those and any
lane compiling them `--arm` was compiling the wrong ISA.  census.py reads the
directive in force at each label and gets the ISA right.

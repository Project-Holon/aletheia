four 2-byte loads per affine group (the PA..PD halfwords at +6/+0xe/+0x16/
+0x1e of each 0x20-byte group).

Structure levers that got it to the right size (508 -> 512 -> 516):
  - the which-dispatch is a `switch`, not if/else-if (two branched-to
    tails, `pop` in the fall-through);
  - the u16 byte offset for the load is a local computed BEFORE the flush
    call (`u16 ofs = off;`), the u32 `off` is kept for the pointer;
  - the affine guard reads alast through `*(volatile u16 *)&s->alast`
    (the image re-reads +0x0c for the count after the guard -- same lever
    as 0x02063378's cursor).
The image's `mov r5,#2; mov r4,r5; mov fp,r5` / `mov r2,#2` pattern in the
affine loop comes out of ../2.0/sp2 on its own from four `fn(.., 2)` calls.

branch: image off r4, n r5, p r6, s r7, 6 r8, fn sb, i sl; compile keeps s
in r5 and shuffles the rest.  Permuter: hill 800 (124 -> 107 via

pad, use(s), temp, hoist-up(fn)).  Writing the two loops as LoadOam()/
LoadAffine() inline helpers is worse (512 bytes, the 0x540 is CSE'd across
the branches).

packs five 32-bit fields through a byte swap into a global device block at

compiles to 372 bytes while the original is 404.  The original does TWO things
the inlined form does not:

    original vs push {r3,r4,r5,lr} when the swaps are inlined), because the
    compiler evaluates all five swaps before their stores and runs out of

them afterwards (the spills are the locals), not as five `g->fX = BSWAP(d->fY)`
assignments.  Both spellings tried (`g->f54 = BSWAP(d->f10)` inline, and a
`static` helper -- which mwcc does NOT inline, it emits `bl bswap`) come out

symbol.  A version with five explicit `u32 b0..b4` locals that is stored after

The axis-(1,1,1) rotation builder called through the four-entry function
pointer table in func_0205429C: it takes (sin, cos, MtxFx43 *) and writes
cos+t on the diagonal, t-s and t+s off it, and zeroes the translation row,
where t = FX_Mul(FX32_ONE - cos, 0x555) and s = FX_Mul(sin, 0x93D).
0x555 is FX32_ONE/3 and 0x93D is FX32_ONE/sqrt(3), so the matrix is the
Rodrigues form for the normalised (1,1,1) axis. Its two siblings at
0x0205650C and 0x020565C8 are the same shape and are blocked the same way.

Everything below is reproduced exactly by the C at the end, under
-O4,p -proc arm7tdmi (see the idiom on this translation unit's -proc): the
rsb against FX32_ONE, the operand order of both umull, the 64-bit +0x800
and >>12 rounding, the cos+t / t-s / t+s roles, and the column-wise store
order 0x00, 0x0C, 0x18, 0x24, 0x04, 0x10, ... which only appears once the
twelve matrix fields are declared volatile (otherwise stm folds 0x04 and
0x08 into one stmib).

Two things do not reproduce, and both are the same finding:

1. The 64-bit multiplier's high word. The original expands the product as
   a full 64x64:

       mov  r4, #0                   ; the constant's high word, in a register
       umull sb, r8, r7, r3
       mla  r8, r7, r4, r8           ; a_lo * b_hi, with b_hi = 0
       asr  r7, r7, #0x1f
       mla  r8, r7, r3, r8           ; a_hi * b_lo

   mwcc folds the a_lo * b_hi term away whenever the multiplier is a
   compile-time constant, in every spelling tried: (fx64)a * 0x555,
   0x555LL * a, (fx64)(u32)0x555, an fx64 local assigned the constant, a
   static const fx64, an fx64 parameter of a static inline helper, and a
   union pun. The only construct that reproduces the three-term form is a
   genuinely non-constant fx64 operand (an out-of-line parameter, or a
   local the compiler cannot trace), which no plausible source provides
   here.

2. The rounding constant. The original materialises mov lr, #0x800 and
   adds it in register form (adds sb, sb, lr) in both expansions; every
   spelling here gives adds rX, rX, #0x800 with the immediate. The same
   thing happens in func_02057EF8, where 0x800 is used three times, so it
   is not a use-count threshold.

Both are systematic across this translation unit rather than specific to
one expression, so they read as a further compiler-configuration
difference beyond the -proc arm7tdmi already established for this unit,
not as a source-shape question. No relocation site is involved; the two
pool words hold the literal constants.

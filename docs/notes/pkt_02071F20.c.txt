pkt_02071F20 - the two callers of the 0x02071CDC constructor family
2026-09-21

Follows docs/notes/pkt_02071DD4.c.txt, which cut this packet and listed
0x02071F20 as "NOT ATTEMPTED (oversized)".  It is not oversized.

SOLVED THIS RUN (EXACT):
  0x02071F20   src/matched/func_02071F20.c   316 B, default 1.1p1,
               no pin needed.

Three levers paid for it, and all three are new to this family:

  L1  THE SCALE FIELDS f30/f34 ARE `volatile`.  Each scale block applies
      the object's scale to three record slots, and the image RE-READS
      the scale from the object before the SECOND and THIRD multiply
      (`ldr r2,[r4,#0x30]` at 0x02071FC4, `ldr r1,[r4,#0x30]` at
      0x02071FDC).  Plain non-volatile C lets 1.1p1 forward the register
      it still holds live and batch all three smulls together: 308 bytes
      against 316, and the whole block reshaped.  Plain `volatile` on the
      field overshoots the other way - it also re-reads for the GUARD
      compare, 332 bytes - so the guard's own load is bound to a local
      `s`, the FIRST multiply uses that local, and only the other two
      re-read through the volatile field.  That exact split is the
      only spelling measured that gives 316.

  L2  THE CALL'S BASE IS A POINTER LOCAL, NOT `&r.f04`.  The image spends
      TWO instructions on the second argument, `add r1, sp, #0` at
      0x02072040 and `add r1, r1, #4` at 0x02072048.  `&r.f04` folds to a
      single `add r1, sp, #4` and the function comes out 4 bytes short.
      Binding `s32 *base = (s32 *)&r;` and passing `base + 1` reproduces
      the pair.  Worth trying anywhere a stack record's body is passed
      just past its header word.

  L3  THE HEADER CONSTANTS ARE ORDERED BY THEIR STORES, NOT THEIR
      DECLARATIONS.  The record's four non-zero header fields are stored
      around a run of nine zero stores, and mwcc emits the four constant
      `mov`s in order of the FIRST store that consumes each, sinking any
      constant whose store sits after the zero run.  Only the source
      order `f04, f48, f40, f44` gives the image's
      `mov r1,#0 / mov r3,#3 / mov r2,#2 / mov r0,#0x1000`.  Measured:
      f44-first is 27 bytes out, f48-first 8, and the three wrong
      orderings differ from the right one by a pure ROTATION of the three
      non-zero constants - a distinctive and easy-to-read signature.
      Declaration order, and whether the constants are bound to locals at
      all, are both insensitive; only the store order reaches it.

The dispatch is `data_020A8458[o->f00 & 7](&r.f08, o)`, a `blx` through a
pointer table; match.py attests the ABS32 site `data_table x16`, so the
table needs no witness work.

NEAR MISS, NOT FILED:

  0x02072068, 340 B, DIFF at 332 B (8 short, 2 instructions), 213
  concrete bytes.  The general member of the family: the s32 2x2 block
  AND the f24/f28 cross terms, so the f30/f34 pair is a full s64
  intermediate multiplied by an s32 (the umull/mla/mla triples at
  0x02072140 and 0x02072164).  This run reached the SAME 332 bytes the
  pkt_02071DD4 lane reached, from a different direction, which is some
  evidence 332 is a real floor for the obvious shapes.

  What is now known about the shape, beyond that note:
  * The pairing is settled and is NOT symmetric.  `hA` = f24*f20 -
    f28*f22 is scaled by f1c and ADDED to the f14 value to make the f34
    term; `hB` = f28*f20 + f24*f22 is scaled by f18 and SUBTRACTED from
    the f18*f20 value to make the f30 term.  Swapping the two pairings
    costs 24 bytes (332 -> 356) and is the single largest lever found.
  * Binding f18/f1c/f20/f22 to named locals before the first block is
    worth 16 bytes (372 -> 356); without it every multiply re-reads.
  * Writing the f30 statement BEFORE the f34 addend `c` is worth a
    further 24 bytes (356 -> 332).  Anything that lengthens `c`'s live
    range - hoisting the `- 0x1000`, computing `c` first, splitting the
    subtract out - puts it straight back to 356.
  * The residue is a stack frame.  The image allocates NO frame: it
    spills exactly one value, the f2c read, into the `push {r3, ...}`
    slot (`str r1,[sp]` at 0x020720AC, reloaded `ldr r0,[sp]` at
    0x0207217C).  Every 332-byte spelling measured emits `sub sp, sp, #8`
    instead and spills two.  Getting the spill count down to one is the
    whole remaining 8 bytes, and reading f2c early into a local does NOT
    do it (measured here and in the earlier note).

  Not a compiler-pin candidate: the shortfall is a spill-count and
  register-pressure difference, not any of the three pinnable tells
  (kept branches, kept constants, re-read/forward).  No alternative front
  end was probed, correctly.

UNLABELLED FOLLOWERS: none new.  Both functions in this packet carry
`func_` labels, and the 12 bytes after 0x02071F20's `pop` are its own
literal pool (0x0207205C 0x00101610, 0x02072060 0x00101810, 0x02072064
0x020A8458), not a follower.

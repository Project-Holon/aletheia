in the second half of the loop body -- the original keeps the loop index
scaled by 2 in r1 and reuses r1 for the second pointer/value, the compiler
gives that run r0:

  +0032  adds r1, r5, r1   | adds r0, r5, r1
  +0034  adds r1, #0xb0    | adds r0, #0xb0
  +0036  ldrh r1, [r1]     | ldrh r0, [r0]

chain and the epilogue are all byte-identical, so this is pure allocation,
not a semantic difference. i is already `int` (the `blt` on the loop back

 1. +0x0048/+0x004C: the image emits `mov r6, #0` then `cmp r0, #0` then
    `popls {...}`, an UNSIGNED `ls` early-out that keeps the induction
    variable initialised ahead of the test. 2.0 emits `cmp r0, #0` then
    `popeq {...}` and sinks the `mov` after it. The note's C spells the
    guard `if (n <= 0) return;` on a u32, which is the `ls`; the extra word
    the image spends is the hoisted `mov`, and 2.0 declines to hoist it.
 2. +0x006C: the image RE-READS `ldrb r0, [sb, #9]` at the top of the loop
    before `cmp r6, r0`, where 2.0 keeps the count live from the entry load
    and compares straight away. That single dropped re-read is the 4 bytes.

volatile-looking byte field each iteration), so this body reads as 1.1p1 and

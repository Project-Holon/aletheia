Two things this C pins down and that are NOT free choices:

  - The buffer declaration order is `a; c; b; d;`, not the address order
    (a at sp+0, b at sp+8, c at sp+0x1c, d at sp+0x3c).  Declared a, b, c, d
    the compiler lays c out at sp+8 and b at sp+0x28 and the two `add rN, sp,
    #imm` setups move -- that is the tell that fixes the declaration order.

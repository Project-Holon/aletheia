emits it only under a `-proc` from the v5+ half of its list; every such `-proc`
returns by popping straight into `pc` and never through `lr`.  The two-instruction
return (`pop {..., lr}; bx lr`) appears only under the ARMv4T `-proc` values
(generic, v4, v4t, arm7tdmi, arm710t/720t/740t, arm9tdmi, arm920t/922t/940t,
dbmx1, dbmxl), every one of which emits `mov lr, pc; bx rN` for an indirect
call instead of `blx`.  Measured over all 32 accepted `-proc` values and all
nine `dsi` builds; the full matrix is in docs/notes/pkt_02053218.c.txt, whose
own function has the identical contradiction (blx at 0x02053EC4 and 0x02054268,
pop-lr returns at 0x02053E74 and 0x02054270).

THREE CODEGEN FACTS FOUND HERE, EACH REPRODUCIBLE IN ISOLATION

  1. An `s64` local materialised BEFORE a switch makes mwcc emit the general
     64x64 multiply inside the arms -- `umull lo, hi, a, b` plus two `mla`
     corrections with each operand's sign extension computed by `asr` --
     where the same product written narrow inside one basic block is a single
     `smull`.  The arms' sequence
       asr lr, r0, #0x1f        (sign of t)
       umull ip, sl, r4, r0     (w * t)
       mla sl, r4, lr, sl       (+ w * sign(t))
       mla sl, r1, r0, sl       (+ sign(w) * t)
     is therefore `(s64)w * (s64)t` with `w64` assigned before the switch
     (which is why `asr r1, r4, #0x1f` sits in the pre-switch block at
     0x02054E58), not a hand-written sign-corrected multiply.  Probe:
     `s64 x = (s32)a; s64 y = b; switch (k) { case 0: return
     (u32)(((x * y) + 0x800) >> 12); }` gives the three-instruction form;
     the same product with no switch and one basic block gives `smull`.
     The 64-bit constant 0x800 is materialised once as a register pair
     (`mov r3, #0x800` and `mov sb, #0` at 0x02054E3C/0x02054E64) and reused
     by every arm, which is why the arms' carries read `adc ..., sb`.
  2. A u16 bitfield read is a SHIFT PAIR, not shift+and.  For
     `typedef struct { u16 b0:5; u16 b5:5; u16 b10:6; } Q2E;` the read
     `((Q2E *)(q+0x2e))->b5` compiles to `ldrh; lsl #22; lsr #27` (lsl by
     31-last_bit, lsr by 32-width), while `(v >> 5) & 0x1f` on the promoted
     value compiles to `mov rN, v, lsr #5; and rN, rN, #0x1f`.  The
     original's 0x02054DD0-0x02054DDC pair is the bitfield form, and the
     3-bit switch field at 0x02054F00 and the 1-bit test at 0x02054F04 are
     the same construct on *(u32*)(res+0x48) and *(u32*)res.  The 16-bit
     field used as `k` is the exception: `(x48 << 8) >> 16` is the unequal
     shift pair a narrowing cast folded with a shift makes (`(u16)(x >> 8)`).
  3. A pointer that is only needed before the calls costs a callee-saved
     register when it is a named local, and 56 bytes of size.  Spelling the
     resource chain out again inside the arms took the transcription from
     1700 to 1756 bytes and from 425 to 439 instructions, because it lets the
     allocator rematerialise the chain instead of keeping it live across the
     six SDK calls.  The original does exactly this: 0x02055078-0x02055084
     and 0x020552F0-0x02055300 both rebuild the chain from `self` (r7) rather
     than reading a register, and the E arm rebuilds it once per position
     component (0x02055184, 0x020551AC, 0x020551F4).  (Rebuilding it for the
     three 0x04000470 stores as well makes things WORSE, 1816 bytes: the
     original keeps the pointer live across that spot.)

The original loads self+0x28 into lr and self+0x2c into r3 (ascending offset,
first value into lr); mwcc 1.1p1 with -O4,p consistently loads self+0x2c into
lr first and self+0x28 into r1, i.e. descending offset and a different scratch.
The instruction sequence apart from those registers is identical, including the
pooled 0xFFCFFFEF mask and `ip`/`ip+0x1000` for 0x04000000 / 0x04001000.

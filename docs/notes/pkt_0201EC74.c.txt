The note's own "next lever" was right and was the first of two.

1. `goto fail;` with ONE `return 0;` at the end of the function, in place of
   four separate `return 0;` statements.  This is the explicit-goto case of
   docs/idioms/guards-separated-by-statements-share-an-exit-only-through-goto.md:
   statements sit between the guards, so nothing but the goto reproduces the
   shared epilogue at 0x0201ECFA.  It alone took the compile from 152 bytes /
   65 differing rows to 144 bytes / 14 differing bytes, and fixed the
   callee-saved swap the note recorded (r5/r7) at the same time.

2. `u8 heap` in place of `u32 heap`.  With the goto chain in place the whole
   remaining residue was the prologue schedule: `ldr r5,[pc]` ahead of
   `adds r7,r0,#0`, `str r1,[sp,#4]` ahead of `str r2,[sp,#8]`, and the
   `str r7,[r6]` / `ldr r0,[sp,#8]` pair at +0022 swapped.  Narrowing the
   third parameter changes nothing that runs -- the value is spilled to sp+8
   untruncated and read back as a word for `p->heap = heap` -- but it changes
   the order mwccarm's value numbering creates the three incoming values in,
   and the prologue schedule follows that order.

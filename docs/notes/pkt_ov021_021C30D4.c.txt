Function: k = func_020808F4() (arm); walks 0xC000 halfwords at 0x06860000 (VRAM), halving each RGB555 channel
(a<<15 | (r>>1)<<10 | (g>>1)<<5 | (b>>1)); then func_0207FDCC(k) (arm). v must be `int` (original uses asrs on
the loaded halfword); channels are u8 locals, halves narrow to u8 again.
Size matches, but the original hoists `movs r7,#1` (the `& 1` mask) into a loop-invariant register with the
counter r5, pointer r4 and bound r6, and computes all four channel extractions from r3 before narrowing;
ours keeps the pointer in r3, bound in r7 and rematerialises `movs r0,#1` in the loop. A local `int m = 1` used
as the mask folds away (identical bytes). Separate u8 locals for the halves versus inline (u8)(x>>1) casts:
identical bytes.

{

        int v = *p;
        u8 r = (v >> 10) & 0x1F;
        u8 g = (v >> 5) & 0x1F;

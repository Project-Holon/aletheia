WHY THE PERMUTER COULD NOT HAVE FOUND THIS.  Worth recording, because 776
distinct compiles returning a perfectly flat score is otherwise a strong
signal that a function is closed.  Every permuter.py mutation except `type`
is value-preserving, and `type` changes the integer type of a local or

is outside the mutation table entirely, so no budget on this body would ever
have reached it.  A flat anneal is evidence that no rearrangement of the
existing statements helps -- it is NOT evidence that the declarations are
right.  When the permuter goes flat on a small residue, re-examine the

0x02098B44 (4 bytes) and 0x02099224 (4 bytes) are NOT functions. Both are a bare
`push {r0, r1, r2, r3}`, the argument home area that belongs to the function the listing starts
4 bytes later: the 1684-byte function labelled 0x02098B48 and the 724-byte one labelled 0x02099228.
No C spelling produces a bare home-area push, and the worklist's 4-byte entries are artifacts of
disasm.py labelling after the prologue. The real starts are 0x02098B44 and 0x02099224, so the pair
is a candidate for layout/function_starts.txt rather than for src/matched/.

The two giants are gated rather than codegen-blocked: 0x02098B48 (1684 bytes) and 0x0209953C
(2112 bytes) each branch into dozens of neighbours that are still open, so no amount of care in
their own bodies can reach EXACT until those land. 0x0209953C also sits next to 0x02099228 (724
bytes), which has no call site anywhere in the image and is presumably reached through a pointer
table.

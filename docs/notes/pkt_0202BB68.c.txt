func_0202BB68 (0x0202BB68, 72 bytes) and func_0202BBB0 (0x0202BBB0, 76
bytes) are STRUCTURAL TWINS and should be worked as a pair.  Both:

  - take (u8 *o, int i);
  - index a 0x1c-stride entry array based at o + 0x24, and return early
    when the entry's first word is zero;
  - call a comparator on that entry, giving a signed s32;
  - load a second s32 from a word array indexed by i * 4 (at o + 0x1e4 for
    0202BB68, o + 0x224 for 0202BBB0 -- both materialised as
    `movs #0x79/#0x89; lsls #2`, which is what fixes the two base offsets);
  - dispatch to one of two helpers on the sign of the difference, each arm
    carrying its own `pop`, with a third fall-through exit.

Only two things differ, and both are confirmed from the image:
  - 0202BBB0's comparator takes THREE arguments, not two: +0x0016..+0x001C
    sets up r1 = o + 0xc AND r2 = o + 0x18 before the bl.  0202BB68 sets
    up r1 = o + 0xc only.  Declaring 0202BCA0 with two parameters loses
    the `adds r2, r5, #0 / adds r2, #0x18` pair outright.
  - the compare polarity is MIRRORED between them: 0202BB68 tests
    `cmp r0, r2` (array value left) then `cmp r2, r0`; 0202BBB0 tests

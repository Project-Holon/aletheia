CORRECTION 1: THE PIN IS STILL RIGHT FOR THIS ROW, it just does not close it.
The note declined the pin on the ground that "there is no byte in this function
that 1.1p1 cannot emit".  That reasoning stands for the CSE, but the two front
ends are NOT equivalent here: with the body below, 1.1p1 gives 360 bytes / 262
concrete out and ../2.0/sp2 gives 360 / 204, and at the size-exact point the
gap is wider still.  The cluster pins ../2.0/sp2 on every non-leaf row
(docs/families/cluster_020640DC.h), so the measurements below are under the pin
and a future attempt should start there.  The note's "356 bytes, 42 concrete
out" probe was measured against the note's own body, which -- see correction 2
-- was four bytes long for an unrelated reason.

CORRECTION 2 (THE NEW FINDING): THE SECOND ARM MUST FALL THROUGH INTO THE
FIRST COPY OF THE DUPLICATED TAIL, NOT RETURN.

The note records that the image WRITES THE FOUR-CALL TAIL OUT TWICE, at
0x02064868..0x02064888 and again at 0x0206488C..0x020648AC, and that both
copies reproduce.  What it does not record is HOW the two copies are reached,
and that is where four of its bytes went:

    arm 1 (kind == 1)   `beq #0x206488c`  -- jumps to the SECOND copy
    arm 2 (otherwise)   falls off its loop into the FIRST copy at 0x02064868,
                        which then FALLS THROUGH into the second copy

There is exactly ONE `pop` in the function, at 0x020648B0.  Writing arm 2's
tail as a block that ends in `return` -- which is the natural C and what the
note's body does -- gives arm 2 its own `pop` and costs 8 bytes (360 against
352).  Deleting that `return` so the second arm's tail falls through into the
shared one takes the row straight from 360/204 to 356/201.  The remaining 4
are the a4 respill store landing above the `cmp` instead of below it.

PERMUTER: SIZE-EXACT, AND IT FOUND THE DECLARATION ORDER.  This is the first
row in this packet where the permuter earned its keep.  From the 356/201
baseline, hill mode (800 budget, --types) reached 352 bytes / 98 concrete out
with three mutations:

    decl-order(n <-> j) ; volatile-add(kind) ; decl-order(n <-> cells)

i.e. the four locals want the order `n, j, i, cells`, not source order, and

352 / 70 with `temp(tmp = (u16)(i + 1))` -- the arm-1 increment lifted into its
own named local -- plus `volatile-drop(kind)`.  A second anneal from the 70-out

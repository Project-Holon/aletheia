Where the diff lives: from the shared "store:" tail's *second* half onward
(final strh of tmp1, loop increment, cmp/blt) the compiled bytes match the
original exactly. Every concrete mismatch is confined to (a) the function's
preamble immediately after the sub_02022D88 call and before the loop body,
and (b) the mirror-lookup block. The original materialises r7 = sp+0xc
(the address of the tmp0 stack slot -- the eventual out-pointer arg to
func_020237B8) once, right after the sub_02022D88 call, and keeps it live
across the whole loop; every C shape tried here instead has the compiler
materialise r7 = 0x12<<6 (0x480, the second array's byte offset) at that

depending on variant and shifts several nearby registers by one slot

artifact in func_02023768's DIFF note).

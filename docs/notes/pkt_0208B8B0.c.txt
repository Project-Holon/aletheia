pkt_0208B8B0 / func_0208B8B0 @ 0x0208B8B0 (144 bytes, ARM) -- DIFF, 9 bytes in the head
=======================================================================================

Two findings, one of them a correction to the lane table.

The lane's `needs` column names 0x02088498 as the blocker.  It is not:
match.py resolves it at [label x40], like every other call in the function.
0x02088498 is the recognised long-branch veneer, so an ordinary extern
declared at the veneer's own address covers it.  What does *not* resolve is
the last pool word, `func_0208B950` at +0x98: 0x0208B950 is the address of
the function immediately after this one and has no label of its own, so
this row caps at MATCH even when every concrete byte is right -- which is
the one thing standing between the body below and a park.

The body (settled; the whole loop, both callback setups and the tail are
byte-identical):

    ORIGINAL                              COMPILED
    ldr  r0, [pc, #0x84]                  ldr  r1, [pc, #0x84]
    ldrh r1, [r0]                         ldrh r0, [r1]
    cmp  r1, #0                           cmp  r0, #0
    ...                                   ...
    strh r4, [r0]                         strh r4, [r1]
    str  r2, [r0, #0x30]                  str  r2, [r1, #0x30]
    ldr  r1, [pc, #0x68]                  ldr  r0, [pc, #0x68]

i.e. mwcc puts the *address* of g_02153000 in r0 and the loaded halfword
in r1 here, and the other way round in every spelling tried.  Nine bytes
of diff, three instructions, no size difference (160 both ways), and the
shift propagates into the `ldr` of the second global and every store off
the first.

Tried, all 160 bytes and all 9 bytes of diff: `if (g.f00 != 0) return;`,
`if (g.f00) return;`, `!= 0u`, `(u32)g.f00 != 0`, a `u16 v = g.f00;` local
compared afterwards, a `u32 v` local, `*(u16 *)&g`, and a
`struct S *p = &g_02153000;` local pointer (that one is worse: 156 bytes
and 48 bytes of diff, because the pointer stays live and forces r5 out of
the loop's constant pool).

Verdict: DIFF, codegen (address register of the leading guard), 9 of 160
concrete bytes, plus the 0x0208B950 pool word which will hold it at MATCH
even after the head is fixed.

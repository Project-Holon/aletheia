WHY THE SIBLING ESCAPES AND THIS ONE DOES NOT: the sibling uses the scratch
THREE times, this one twice.  At three uses mwccarm prefers to rematerialise
the pool load at each site; at two it caches the address in a callee-saved
register and spends two `adds r0, r4, #0`.  That is a cost-model threshold in
the allocator, not something the C selects, which is why lever 1 -- which is
exactly the "make the address expression fresh per site" change -- moves the
sibling's byte count and not this one's size.

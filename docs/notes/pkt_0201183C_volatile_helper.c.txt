WHERE THE 20 BYTES ACTUALLY ARE.  The `--diff` divergence begins at
**+0x0008**, in the prologue, long before the first _fadd.  The nine
loop blocks are as byte-exact as this note claims; what is 20 bytes
long is the six-flag preamble that computes `tot` and `k`.  Read the

  * `tot` lives in r7 and `k` in r6 for the whole preamble, both in

    f2c -> r0 (+0x24, +0x46), f28 -> r1 (+0x30).  The compile re-reads
    every flag at all six sites.

THE LEVER, worth 16 bytes: FOLD EACH FLAG'S TWO EFFECTS INTO ONE `if`.

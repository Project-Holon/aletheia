WHAT IT IS
----------
The third of the blit family reached from the +0x1c kind byte, and the odd
one out: its two neighbours func_020426A0 and func_02042B24 are FILLS, which
write a constant into a rectangle of the destination tilemap.  This one is a
COPIER -- it reads from a second surface and writes what it reads.  That is
what the eleven parameters and the `mla` at +0x30 are for.

The structure, all of it established this pass and all of it settled:

  * ELEVEN PARAMETERS: four in registers (r0 the destination Screen, r1, r2,
    r3) and seven on the stack, read at [sp,#0x50], #0x54, #0x58, #0x5c,
    #0x60, #0x64 and #0x68.  The three re-stored immediately
    (`ldr r0,[sp,#0x50]; str r0,[sp,#0x50]`) are the ones whose home slot
    the compiler reuses; that is a homing artefact, not eleven distinct
    values being rewritten.

  * THE SECOND PARAMETER STAYS IN A REGISTER, THE THIRD IS SPILLED.  The
    prologue is `mov sb, r1` then `str r2, [sp]`, so whichever of the
    destination coordinates is passed second is the one in sb.  Writing them
    in the other order swaps two instructions at +0x10 and costs nothing in
    size, so this is the one parameter fact the diff names directly rather
    than by consequence.

  * THE LAST PARAMETER IS u8: `ldrb r0, [sp, #0x68]`, not `ldr`.  (Contrast
    with func_02042D2C, where the same load is `ldr` and the parameter is
    STILL u8 -- there the width came out of the spill cost, not the load.
    Here the `ldrb` settles it outright.)

  * TWO WHOLE LOOP NESTS, selected by that byte, sharing nothing.  The
    nests are the same shape and differ only in how the SOURCE index is
    computed: the first nest multiplies out by hand
    (`mla r6, r7, r2, r1`, i.e. src + sw * row), the second calls
    func_02040F84 per cell.  Both nests index the DESTINATION through
    func_02040EE0, the same helper func_02042D2C and func_02042ED8 use.

  * THE GUARDS ARE CONJUNCTIONS, NOT SEPARATE `if`s.  The image computes
    both bounds tests and then predicates -- `cmp r0, r2 ; ldrlt r2, [sp,#0x10]
    ; ... ; cmplt r2, r1 ; bge <exit>` -- with ONE exit for the pair.  Two
    separate `if`s emit two exits and cost 4 bytes plus a reshuffle; this is
    docs/idioms/guard-chain-sharing-one-exit-block-is-a-single-conjunction.md.
    Writing the conjunction rather than two ifs took the best from 588/127
    to 584/106, which is the single largest step this pass.

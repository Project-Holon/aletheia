The structure, all of it established this pass and all of it settled:

  * ELEVEN PARAMETERS: four in registers (r0 the destination Screen, r1, r2,
    r3) and seven on the stack, read at [sp,#0x50], #0x54, #0x58, #0x5c,

    destination coordinates is passed second is the one in sb.  Writing them

Every spelling under 1.1p1 keeps them in callee-saved registers instead
(`adds r5, r2, #0` / `adds r4, r3, #0`), which costs one extra push register,

bytes, which is the whole size gap.  The residue is that spill decision and
nothing else; the argument VALUES, their slots and every branch target agree.

instruction count, not evidence, and the concrete residue only drops from 30

that spelling and think it is close.

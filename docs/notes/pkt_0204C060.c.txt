WHAT MOVED THE NEEDLE, 194 -> 60, and is the single most important fact here:

  THE MASK 0x00C000C0 IS ONE NAMED LOCAL USED TWICE.  The image materialises
  the pool word into r0 at 0x0204C2D8 and then uses it BOTH as the mask
  (`and r5, r3, r0` at 0x0204C2F0) AND, shifted, as the OR operand of the
  first clamp (`orrgt r2, r6, r0, asr #22` at 0x0204C2EC; 0x00C000C0 >> 22
  == 3).  Writing the clamp as `a | 3` makes the two independent, costs the
  pool word its second use and shifts 134 further bytes.  Write:

      u32 k = 0x00C000C0;
      s32 m = *x & k;
      ...
      a2 = a;
      if (a > 0xff) a2 = a | ((s32)k >> 22);

  The unconditional `a2 = a` before the guard is what gives the image's
  `mov r2, r6` at 0x0204C2E4; an if/else gives an extra `movle`.

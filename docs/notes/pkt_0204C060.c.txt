THE ACTUAL CURE was already written down, in a sibling: src/matched/func_0204C060.c
carries it as its third lever.  The u8 narrowing must be applied to the MASK,
not to the shifted value:

    s32 m6 = (s32)(u8)(w & 0x3f);
    s32 v  = (m6 << 2) & 0xff;

Every spelling that narrows the SHIFTED value lets 1.1p1 prove the field
contiguous and fuse it.  Measured this pass, all exactly 6 out:
`(u8)((w & 0x3f) << 2)`; `u32 m = w & 0x3f; (u8)(m << 2)`;
`(u8)((u32)(w & 0x3f) << 2)`; `u32 m = (w & 0x3f) << 2; m & 0xff`;
`u8 v = (w & 0x3f) << 2`; `(u8)(lo * 4)` with `lo` a separate u32 local.
`w` declared u32 gives 7 (the `asr #11` becomes `lsr`), `w` declared s32 gives
6, `((w & 0x3f) << 2) & 0xff` in one expression gives 132.

from the mask it already holds, not a "kept 3".  All the fold-defeating
levers measured above were chasing a misread constant.

{
    if (x > 0xff)
        x |= ~0xff;

}

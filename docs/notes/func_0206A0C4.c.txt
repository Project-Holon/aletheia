func_0206A0C4 -- near miss, DIFF, one instruction and a leaf frame (76 vs 80).

0x0206A0C4 (76 bytes, no relocations) detaches one or two buffers: for each of
(outA, outB) that is non-null it clears bit 0 of a 16-bit status word at
+0x10/+0x20, hands the 32-bit block pointer at +0x08/+0x18 to the caller and
nulls the field.

The 1-bit-bitfield spelling of the clear (`u16 f10_0 : 1`) is the one that
reproduces the instruction set exactly, `bic ip, ip, #1` included -- the plain
`u16 f10 &= ~1` form instead loads a pooled 0xFFFFFFFE and `and`s, 92 bytes.
With the bitfield the compiled body is instruction-for-instruction identical to
the original, but mwcc hoists the `ldr ip, [r0, #8]` of the block pointer above
the `strh` that writes the status word, and that hoist makes the mask and the
pointer live at once, so the allocator reaches for lr and the leaf grows a
`push {r3, lr}` / `pop {r3, pc}` frame: 20 instructions against 19.

Tried and rejected: `&= 0xfffe`, `= x & 0xfffe`, `(u16)(x & ~1)`, a `u16`
local, `volatile` on the status words, `char *` casts for both accesses (to
break the alias-based hoist), and taking `u16 *st = &p->f10` into a local.
All land on the same 80/92-byte shapes.

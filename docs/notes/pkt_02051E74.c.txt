func_02051F34 @ 0x02051F34, 328 bytes, ARM, --cc 1.1p1 -O4,p.
NOT the 1.2 pin: this is a PART 1 math helper of cluster_02051E64 and
dsi/1.1p1 is right for it (see docs/families/cluster_02051E64.h).

WHAT IT IS: a segment-versus-sphere intersection test.  Given a segment
(seg->p0 at +0x00, seg->p1 at +0x0c) and a ball (centre at +0x00, radius at
+0x0c), it projects the centre onto the segment --
t = FX_Div(dot(d, c - p0), dot(d, d)) where d = p1 - p0 -- and takes one of
two paths:

  * 0 <= t <= 0x1000 (the projection lands ON the segment): scale d by t,
    subtract, measure, and compare the distance against the ball radius
    PLUS the word at seg+0x18.  That extra term is the `ldr r1,[r6,#0x18]`
    / `add r1,r2,r1` pair at +00A8/+00B0 and is easy to miss: without it
    the arm tail-merges with the else and the function is 32 bytes short.
  * otherwise (the projection is off either end): measure to p1 first, and
    only if that misses, measure to p0.

The result is written into a 20-byte out-struct at sp+0x24 -- { u32 hit;
Vec3 normal; int dist; } -- which is copied to the caller's buffer with the
`ldm r5!,{r0-r3}; stm r4!,{r0-r3}; ldr; str` quintet at +0128..+0134 when
the third argument is non-null, and `hit` is returned.

WHAT FIXED THE FRAME (0x38, nine registers pushed):
The three out-values must be ONE declared struct at the top of the frame,
not three separate locals.  Written separately the compiler packs the frame
to 0x34 and the ldm/stm copy becomes field-by-field moves: 296 bytes, 32
short.  Declared as a struct with the locals AFTER it in the order
sd, bc, tmp -- the later declaration takes the LOWER stack address, per
docs/idioms/local-declaration-order-is-stack-order.md -- the frame, the
push list and every `add rN, sp, #k` immediate come out right in one go.

WHAT FIXED THE SIZE (328 exactly):
Three temporaries, all found by tools/permuter.py --mode hill and none of
them obvious: bind dot(d, d), bind dot(d, c - p0) and bind b->r.  The two
dot products must each be a NAMED LOCAL before FX_Div rather than inline
arguments -- this is docs/idioms/cmp-operand-order-names-the-temporary.md
in its call-argument form, and it is worth 4 bytes and the whole of the
+0044..+0068 schedule.

THE RESIDUE: 56 concrete bytes at 328/328 after
`--mode anneal --budget 1500 --jobs 8 --types`, down from 72 at the
hand-written point.  It is register allocation across the two arms, not
shape: the branch targets, the call order, the frame and the stack offsets

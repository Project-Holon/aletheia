WHAT IT IS
----------
The rare random event that seeds the packed byte field id 0x97, whose readers
are src/matched/unit_0201B220.c and whose other writers are
src/matched/unit_0201B100.c.  It draws a 16 bit random number, fires only on
three of the 65536 values, picks a party slot at random, rejects empty slots
and eggs and slots that already have the field set, then rolls a second random
byte and packs it into the field.

VERSION SENSITIVITY
-------------------
No species, item or move id, but the probabilities are content:

  0x4000, 0x8000, 0xC000   the three of 65536 draws that fire.  They are three
                           exact values, not a range, so the event is a 3 in
                           65536 chance per call.
  7                        the mask the second roll has to be non zero under,
                           and the value it is reduced to when the high nibble
                           is also set.
  0xF3                     the mask the packed byte is written through, i.e.
                           bits 2 and 3 are cleared.
  1                        the increment, so the field is never written zero.
  0x97, 5, 0x4C            party record ids: the field, the species and the
                           egg flag, in src/matched/sub_02018DB4.c's space.

Four details are settled and worth not re-deriving.

* Every random draw in the file is a multiply-high, never a shift or a modulo:
  see docs/idioms/random-below-n-is-a-64-bit-multiply-high.md.  The three here
  are `* 0x10000`, `* n` and `* 0x100`, and only the middle one reaches
  `_ull_mul`, because the other two are powers of two and mwccarm expands them
  inline.
* The `cmp r1, #0` guard in front of the `_ull_mul` call is the inlined body of
  the "random below n" helper, not a division-by-zero check invented by the
  compiler: with n zero the product would be zero rather than the raw draw, so
  the guard is in the source.
* The three equality tests are one disjunction.  Written as three separate
  `if`s each gets its own copy of the epilogue
  (docs/idioms/disjunction-must-be-one-expression-to-share-its-exit.md).
* The second roll binds `v & 7` to a local: the original computes it once into
  r2, tests it for the loop condition, and reuses it in the
  `if ((v & 0xF0) != 0)` arm.  Recomputing `v & 7` in the arm costs an
  instruction.

The original copies the rolled byte into a third register before the `orrs`,
so the OR accumulates into a register that is not one of its operands' homes,
and it lets the final truncation pair straddle the argument set-up.  Every
source shape tried accumulates into the shift's own temporary and keeps the
pair together, which is two bytes shorter.

What was tried, so that nobody repeats it.  About sixty shapes, generated and
scored by match.py's own diff count:

* both operand orders of the `|`, the shifted half bound to its own local, the
  result assigned through `w = v; w |= ...`, and the whole thing written over
  `v` instead of a second variable.  The ranking is completely flat at 28,
  which is
  docs/idioms/the-accumulator-of-a-commutative-chain-is-its-second-operand.md
  failing to bite: here the accumulator is neither operand;
* `w` declared at all seven positions in the local list and as u8, u16, u32 and
  s32.  `u8` loses the truncations, the rest are identical;
* the `& 0xF3` as a compound assignment, as a spelled out assignment and with
  or without the cast; the `+ 1` as `w++`, `w = w + 1` and `(u8)(w + 1)`; and
  the final truncation moved to the call argument, which splits the pair the
  right way but costs four bytes elsewhere;
* four prototypes for func_02017E40's third parameter.

The flat ranking says the cause is a live range rather than a spelling: the
original keeps the rolled byte alive across the OR, and nothing in the visible
tail needs it.  The natural next thing to look for is a use of `v` after the
write that the reconstruction has folded away, which would also explain why the
truncation is allowed to sink.

ATTESTATION
-----------
Not a blocker, but worth recording: 0x0201B220 is reached only from the `bl` at
0x0201B056 inside this function, so a standalone range for it has no `label`
witness.  Since src/matched/unit_0201B220.c is an EXACT entry the `matched`
class carries the site, and the diff above was taken before that entry was
written, which is why the harness printed it unresolved.

Both are the same family: look an object up (func_02074324), bail out with 0 if
the lookup fails, then hand the object's three header fields to func_02075698.
0x02075560 is the two-argument form and passes `*(u8*)(p+9)`, `*(u16*)(p+4)`,
`*(u8*)(p+8)` straight through; 0x020755A8 is the six-argument form where the
2nd/3rd/4th parameters are *defaulted* from those same fields when they arrive
negative:

    if (d < 0) d = *(u8 *)(p + 8);
    if (c < 0) c = *(u16 *)(p + 4);
    if (b < 0) b = *(u8 *)(p + 9);

The parameter must be `int` for the compare to be `cmp r4, #0` / `ldrblt`
signed, which is what the image has.  Both close on the first try with only the
`symbols:` block and the right pointer types.

WHAT THIS NOTE HAD RIGHT ABOUT 0x020754C4, and it is all of the shape: the
signedness conclusions are required and unchanged.  `p[0] <= 0` and the inner
`for` must promote the byte to `int` (the image has `ble` at 0x020754DC and
`blt` at 0x02075548), and the inner counter must be declared `int`, or both
compares come out `bls`/`blo`.  What the note diagnosed as "this front end
pushes two more registers" was the front end: under the pin the frame is the
image's `push {r4, r5, r6, r7, r8, lr}` exactly, the size is exactly 156, and
every branch target, every load offset and every instruction reproduces.

0x020754C4: WHAT IS LEFT IS A FOUR-REGISTER PERMUTATION, 14 bytes.

Two levers took it from 21 bytes out to 14 and both are worth keeping:

  1. DECLARATION ORDER (docs/idioms/declaration-order-decides-which-register-a-local-gets.md).
     `Slot *s; int i; int j;` beats `int i; int j; Slot *s;` by 7 bytes.  Three
     of the four orders measured give 14, the fourth gives 21.
  2. THE INNER COUNTER IS INITIALISED FROM A SEPARATE ZERO VARIABLE, not from
     a literal.  The image materialises TWO zeros at 0x020754D0/0x020754D4 --
     `mov r6, #0` then `mov r5, r6` -- and r5 is used for nothing but
     `mov r8, r5` at 0x02075514, the inner counter's reload each outer
     iteration.  Spelling it `z = i; ... for (j = z; ...)` reproduces that pair
     and fixes r7 and r8; a literal `for (j = 0; ...)` does not.

The residue after both is a 3-cycle over the remaining callee-saved registers:

    slot        original   compiled
    parameter   r4         r5
    outer i     r6         r4
    zero z      r5         r6

Tried and rejected, all landing at 14:

    baseline: 39 single mutations, "no single mutation improves".
  - tools/permuter.py `--mode anneal --budget 60 --jobs 8 --types`: 60 compiles,
    unchanged.  (On the 21-byte baseline, `--mode hill --budget 24`: 23 tried,
    also unmoved -- the declaration lever is outside the mutation table.)
  - all four declaration orders of the three locals, and all four of the four
    once `z` is added.
  - the parameter copied to its own named local at the top of the body.
  - `z = 0; i = z;` instead of `i = 0; z = i;`.
  - the two guards rewritten as `continue`s instead of nesting.

Per docs/notes/permuter.md's own guidance ("a residue the permuter cannot move
at all is usually in the declarations or in the reading of the image"), the
declarations have now been swept exhaustively and the reading is confirmed by
the size and by every instruction matching, so this is a genuine allocation
tie.  Note also that this function is MATCH-capped regardless even if closed:
func_02072DD0 and func_02072DE8 have no caller outside it, as the note already

Under the cluster pin this is 252 bytes against the original's 252 with 5
concrete bytes out.  The one lever that mattered was declaration order:
`u32 wave; u32 bank;` puts bank at sp+4 and wave at sp+8 the way the image has
them (`add ip, sp, #4` at 0x020756D8 for the first call, `add ip, sp, #8` at
0x02075714 for the second), and the reverse order costs 9 bytes rather than 5
(docs/idioms/local-declaration-order-is-stack-order.md, the later declaration
at the lower address).

The residue is the two temporaries of the func_020733E8 call, and it is one
swapped pair:

    02075748 (+00B0)  ldr r2, [sp, #8]     | ldr r3, [sp, #8]
    0207574C (+00B4)  ldr r3, [sp, #4]     | ldr r2, [sp, #4]
    02075750 (+00B8)  ldr r1, [r2, #0x18]  | ldr r1, [r3, #0x18]
    02075758 (+00C0)  add r1, r2, r1       | add r1, r3, r1
    0207575C (+00C4)  mov r2, #0           | mov r3, #0

i.e. the image numbers the wave cell before the bank cell inside that one
statement, and this C numbers them the other way.  Every other byte of the
function -- the seven-register push, the `sub sp, #0xc` frame, all seven calls,
both `func_020733DC` release paths, the `lsl`/`lsr` pair that is NOT present
here (this callee takes the raw word) -- reproduces.

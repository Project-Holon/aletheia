Reading: the original gives the three field loads r1, r0, r2.  Because r0 is
still holding the second value, the CRC call's argument copies cannot be
scheduled above the store that frees it, so they land between the second and
third stores and the third store follows them.  The pinned compiler instead
pre-assigns r0/r1 to the call's arguments and lets the second and third
temporaries share r2 (their live ranges do not overlap in the front end's own
order), after which the scheduler hoists the argument copies as early as they
fit.  Same instruction count, same bytes except five.

~30 spellings were compiled, all with -O4,p / dsi/1.1p1, and every one produced
byte-identical output (the same five-byte residue), which is what says the
allocation is decided before the source shape can reach it:

  - three stores as byte-offset casts off a u8 *; as u32[], u16[] and u16*+7
    subscripts; through a named struct's fields; off two pointers (u32 *d and
    u16 *cr); through a single u32-typed variable holding the address
  - the three values bound to one reused local, three separate locals, a local
    array and a local struct, in several declaration orders
  - each field load hoisted or deferred individually; the third value loaded
    before the second store
  - the CRC store through a u16 * computed before, after or inline
  - two, three and four parameters, including the four-int p0..p3 form
    src/matched/func_02031EE8.c uses; `buf` declared void * / u32 / u8 *
  - a third (unused) argument on the CRC call, and a memory barrier after it

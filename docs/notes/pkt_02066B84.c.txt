Two distinct causes, both cheap to fix next time:

  1. `ldm r6, {r4, r5}` at 0x02066B94 is one load of BOTH dwords.  That is
     the aggregate-read tell: the source reads one struct/array of two words
     (`a[0]`/`a[1]` in one expression, e.g. a local `struct {u32 v, w;} s = a[0..1]`
     or `u32 v = a[0], w = a[1];` with the compiler contracting the pair).
     The attempt's two scalar `a[0]`/`a[1]` loads do not contract.
  2. The mask 0xc000c000 is loaded once at 0x02066B90 and applied to the
     loaded dword; the attempt restructured the extraction into plain shifts
     (`lsr r5, r4, #0xe`), losing the `and` pairs.  The source must mask with
     the 32-bit constant before shifting, keeping `v & 0xC000C000` in a named
     temporary.

The indirect call needs its own declaration; as written the symbol is not an
address-bearing name.  Declare the dword at 0x020A181C in `symbols:` and cast
the value at the blx site, i.e. `((void (*)(u32, u32*, u32*, u32*, u32, u32))
data_020A181C[0])(...)`, so the pool word is an ABS32 of an attested address
rather than a second, unattested symbol.

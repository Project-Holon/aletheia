THE LEVER THAT MATTERED MOST
----------------------------

1.1p1: the shared tail (the four `ldr r0..r3, [r3,#0x20..0x30]` + the `bl`)
is emitted once and both arms branch to it.  That costs 108 bytes across the
three cases and is what held the first attempts at 452 bytes.  Writing the
zero arm as an `if (...) { ...; return; }` arm and the other arm as the
fall-through code after it (instead of `else`) defeats the merge and gives
568 bytes with the original's two `bl func_0207C408` at +0xCC and +0x100.
`goto` to a per-case label is byte-identical to the early-return form.

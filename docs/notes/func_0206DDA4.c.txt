The residue, two decisions that go together:

  1. The original never spends a callee-saved register on &g_0214BAB4.  It
     pushes {r4,r5,r6,lr} and rematerialises the pool word three times:

         0206DDA8  ldr r3, [pc, #0xd0]     ; &g, for the head
         0206DE14  ldr r0, [pc, #0x64]     ; again, after func_02082B0C
         0206DE40  ldr r0, [pc, #0x38]     ; again, in the flush arm

     Every compile pushes {r3,r4,r5,r6,r7,lr}, loads the pool word once into
     r7 and keeps it live across the call.

  2. The original re-reads g_0214BAB4.unk_04 at 0x0206DE44 instead of
     reusing the value it tested at 0x0206DDC4; every compile CSEs the two,
     which is legal (no store between them on either path into the flush
     arm) and is what makes the extra register worth allocating.

Together those are the whole 12-byte difference (228 against 216): two
rematerialised pool loads and the wider push/pop.

What was tried:

  * caching the list pointer in a local at the top, and not caching it at
    all (reading g_0214BAB4.list at every use).  Byte-identical output: the
    compiler's own CSE arrives at the same value numbering either way, so
    this lever does not reach the decision.
  * a local for the copy destination versus the two-level expression
    &g_0214BAB4.list->cmds[g_0214BAB4.list->count].  No change.
  * spelling the global as a cast constant,
    `#define g_0214BAB4 (*(struct GxState *)0x0214BAB4)`, on the theory that
    a literal address is rematerialised where a relocation is not.  This is
    strictly worse: it still allocates a callee-saved register for the base
    and additionally renames every parameter register (r4/r5/r6 become
    r5/r6/r7), so the cast-constant reading of the pool word is wrong.

By the taxonomy in
docs/idioms/a-materialised-shift-temporary-is-not-reachable-from-c-or-from-flags.md
this is the allocator case, with the twist that here it also changes the
instruction count.  The lever that would move it is a way to stop the
compiler CSEing the two `unk_04` tests, and nothing expressible in the
source does that: there is genuinely no store between them.  `volatile` on
the field would force the reload but also force reloads at the two other
sites, which the original does not have.

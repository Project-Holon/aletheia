NOT ATTEMPTED AS C.  Structural and content map only.

This is the evolution checker itself, the largest function in pkt_0201B038 and
the single most version-divergence-prone routine in the territory.  It was read
out of the bytes and mapped rather than reconstructed: at 1116 bytes with two
nested jump tables and about forty content constants it is a wave of work on
its own, and the map below is what that wave needs so that it does not start
from the disassembly again.

WHY IT IS ALSO ATTESTATION-BLOCKED
----------------------------------
Even a byte perfect reconstruction caps at MATCH as the image stands.  The
`bl` at 0x0201B360 passes 0x020A7330 to the debug allocator, and those bytes
spell "shinka_check.c", which is the first direct evidence of this file's
name.  0x020A7330 appears in exactly one literal pool in the 3.9 MB image, the
pool word at 0x0201B5D4 inside this function, so the `literal` class discards
its only witness; and the `string` class refuses as well, because the sixteen
bytes in front of the string are a static table rather than a string:

  020A731C  6C 2E 63 00 | CB 00 9B 00 C3 00 C4 00 C5 00 C3 00
  020A732C  C4 00 C5 00 | 73 68 69 6E 6B 61 ...
                          ^ 020A7330

The single NUL at 0x020A732F satisfies condition 3 of the rule and condition 4
holds, but condition 5 fails: the byte before it is 0xC5, not the last
character of a printable run.  That is exactly the case
docs/idioms/a-string-after-a-data-table-has-no-witness.md records for "mail.c"
and "wifilist.c", and it is right to refuse.  No cut helps, because the range
cannot be smaller than the one function that passes the pointer.  It closes if
anything else in the image is ever found to load 0x020A7330.

The allocation itself is 0x2A bytes at line 106, which puts this function near
the top of shinka_check.c.

SHAPE
-----
Seven parameters, which is why the prologue re-homes two of them:

    sub_0201B2B0(r0, mon, context, param,
                 [sp+0x60] u8, [sp+0x64] u16 *methodOut, [sp+0x68] heap)

`methodOut` is allowed to be NULL and is redirected to a local at sp+0x48 when
it is (0x0201B346).  The `ldr`/`str` pairs at 0x0201B2B6 and 0x0201B2BC that
read a stack parameter and write it back to its own slot are the dead-store
behaviour of
docs/idioms/a-dead-store-to-a-spilled-local-survives-when-the-second-assignment-recomputes.md,
not homing; the same shape recurs at 0x0201B5F6 for the two species constants.

The body is a 0x2A byte record read through sub_0201B764
(src/matched/unit_0201B70C.c), i.e. seven six byte evolution rows, walked by a
`for` whose bound is the literal 7, with the whole walk inside a four arm
switch on `context`:

    context 0   0x0201B388   level up, twenty two methods
    context 1   0x0201B5F6   trade, three methods
    context 2   0x0201B6A2   item use, three methods
    context 3   0x0201B6A2   the same block as context 2

The level up arm holds the second jump table, `cmp r0, #0x1C` and 29 signed
halfwords at 0x0201B3D4, and its entries for 0, 5, 6, 7, 8, 17 and 18 point at
the arm's exit.  Those are genuine gaps in the middle of the range and not
empty case labels, because the range is set from 0 by the `cmp` and the real
arms start at 1; the distinction is
docs/idioms/empty-switch-arms-are-what-make-the-table.md, and it matters here
because ids 5 to 8, 17 and 18 are exactly the methods the other three contexts
handle.

-------------------------------

  four contexts, and each context's arm is a separate copy of it;

* the entry will still be MATCH until 0x020A7330 acquires a witness, so it
